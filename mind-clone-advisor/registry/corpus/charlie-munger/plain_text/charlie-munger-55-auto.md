During a talk at Harvard in 1995 titled The Psychology of Human Misjudgment , Munger mentioned Tupperware parties and open outcry auctions, where he explained "three, four, five of these things work together and it turns human brains into mush," [ 28 ] meaning that normal people will be highly likely to succumb to the multiple irrational tendencies acting in the same direction.
